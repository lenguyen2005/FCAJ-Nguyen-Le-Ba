Event 1

Reflection Report – “FCAJ Weekly Meetup - 13/06/2026”

Purpose of the Event

FCAJ Community Day - 13/06/2026 was a technology community event where experienced speakers shared practical insights and real-world experiences from the industry through topics such as DevOps, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Data Analytics, and System Management.

The speakers’ presentations helped me gain a deeper understanding of real working environments in technology companies while broadening my perspective on future career development.

Speakers

The event featured several experienced professionals from the technology industry:

  • Mr. Trong H. Truong — DevOps Engineer at Endava Vietnam.
  • Mr. Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị — AI Engineer, AWS Community Builder, and AWS Student Builder Group Leader.
  • Mr. Đạt Phạm — Data Analytics Engineer.
  • Mr. Cường Nguyễn — Process Engineer.
  • Mr. Trung Kiên and Mr. Minh Thọ.

Key Highlights

The event covered a wide range of practical topics that closely reflected real-world business challenges and current technology trends.

DevOps in the Real World

Mr. Trong H. Truong discussed several common misconceptions about DevOps, particularly the belief that DevOps is simply about writing CI/CD pipelines or deploying applications.

In reality, DevOps is a mindset focused on optimizing the entire software development and operations lifecycle. A DevOps Engineer must understand the complete system architecture, always ask “Why?” before “How?”, and possess excellent communication and collaboration skills across different teams.

The speaker also emphasized the role of AI in modern software engineering. AI should be treated as a tool that enhances productivity and supports critical thinking rather than replacing human reasoning entirely.

From Student to AWS Community Builder

Mr. Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị shared his inspiring journey from being a participant in the First Cloud AI Journey program to becoming an AWS Community Builder and an AWS Partner representative.

One of the most inspiring topics was the sustainable community development model, where learners progress through several stages:

  • Building curiosity and a learning mindset.
  • Gaining experience through real-world projects.
  • Developing a professional portfolio.
  • Giving back to the community by sharing knowledge and mentoring newcomers.

This model encourages students not only to acquire technical knowledge but also to develop practical experience while contributing to the community.

Data Analytics and Corporate Culture in Multinational Companies

Mr. Đạt Phạm and Mr. Cường Nguyễn shared practical experiences in data analytics from multinational companies.

Some real-world examples included:

  • Building operational dashboards and analyzing GMV performance at Kamereo.
  • Analyzing IoT production data at Colgate-Palmolive to optimize operational costs and improve manufacturing efficiency.

Besides technical expertise, the speakers highlighted four essential skills that distinguish outstanding professionals:

  • Critical thinking.
  • Communication skills.
  • Data storytelling.
  • Problem-solving.

Another interesting topic was the Five Stages of Professional Growth, consisting of:

  1. Follower.
  2. Learner.
  3. Problem Solver.
  4. System Thinker.
  5. Super Star.

According to the speakers, the long-term goal of every engineer should be more than simply completing assigned tasks. They should develop systems thinking, evaluate risks, estimate costs, and understand the business impact before making technical decisions.

The speakers also introduced the No-Blame Post-Mortem culture commonly adopted in modern technology companies. Instead of blaming individuals after incidents occur, organizations focus on identifying root causes and continuously improving the system to prevent similar failures.

Additionally, they shared the philosophy of “Doing the Right Things”, which is built upon three core values:

  • Being a Good Person (Fulfillment).
  • Pursuing Meaningful Work (Purpose & Meaningful).
  • Contributing to Society (Community & Legacy).

This philosophy encourages professionals to pursue not only career success but also meaningful contributions to the community and society.

URL Shortening System Deployed on AWS

The event also included a session introducing the design and deployment of a URL shortening system on AWS. The presentation demonstrated how AWS cloud services can be integrated to build a scalable, highly available, and cost-effective application while applying cloud architecture best practices.

What I Learned

After attending the event, I gained many valuable lessons that will benefit both my studies and my future career.

Understanding DevOps and Using AI Effectively

I learned that DevOps is far more than deploying applications or building CI/CD pipelines. It is about optimizing the entire software delivery process to help development and operations teams work more efficiently.

I also realized the importance of mastering the fundamentals—such as Linux, computer networking, and programming—before relying on automation tools.

Furthermore, AI should be considered a thinking assistant that improves productivity rather than something that completely replaces human reasoning.

Data-Driven Thinking

I learned how organizations use data to make informed decisions instead of relying solely on intuition.

By analyzing dashboards, KPIs, and business metrics, engineers can identify the root causes of problems and propose solutions that improve operational efficiency.

In addition, I realized that Data Storytelling is an essential skill for communicating technical findings clearly and persuasively.

Developing Systems Thinking and Professional Culture

One of the most valuable lessons I learned was the importance of becoming a System Thinker.

Rather than focusing only on completing individual tasks, engineers should understand the entire system, evaluate risks, estimate costs, consider system performance, and assess the broader business impact of every technical decision.

I also gained a better appreciation for continuous learning, professional working culture, and the importance of complying with international standards such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR to ensure data security and information protection.

Personal Reflection

The speakers not only shared technical knowledge but also conveyed professional working mindsets, practical experiences, and valuable career guidance in the technology industry.

For me, this event was not only an opportunity to stay updated with the latest technology trends and solutions but also a chance to reflect on my own career path. It reinforced the importance of building a strong technical foundation, developing systems thinking, embracing lifelong learning, and continuously contributing meaningful value to the technology community and the digital transformation of Vietnam.
